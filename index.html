Volunteer Recruitment Playbook

No calendar. No orientation. No assigning activities yet. This is the simple routine for building the volunteer list from 15 toward 100–150.

What to do every day

Repeat this routine daily. Keep it simple and track what actually produces signups.

1 · Post

Make one public recruitment post

Rotate short video, photo, campaign update, FAQ, progress update, or a simple volunteer signup invitation. Always make the signup link easy to find.

2 · Follow up

Contact new volunteer leads

Reply to new signups and people who asked for information. Confirm their contact information and keep them on the list. Do not assign an activity yet.

3 · Share

Put the opportunity in public places

Share the public volunteer page in appropriate broad community channels that allow political/campaign posts. Avoid spam and follow each group’s rules.

4 · Physical outreach

Flyers + QR cards

Ask permission to place recruitment flyers on community bulletin boards, local-business boards, cafés or other permitted notice areas. Never assume a public building or private business allows political material.

5 · Track

Update the volunteer list

Record new name, contact, date joined, source of signup, replied/pending status, and last follow-up. Remove duplicates.

6 · Measure

Check the numbers

At the end of the day record: inquiries, completed signups, replies, and which source produced them. Put more effort into sources that actually work.

Where to ask about posting flyers

  • Independent cafés and local shops: ask the manager whether they have a community notice board and whether political recruitment notices are allowed.
  • Community recreation centres: contact NVRC and ask about Policy 102 (Advertising, Signage and Information Display) before posting anything.
  • Libraries: ask staff first. Do not assume library notice boards accept campaign material; North Vancouver City Library restricts promotional literature associated with room rentals to the booked room.
  • Apartment/strata notice boards: obtain permission from management/strata before posting.
  • Community events: ask organizers whether flyers or QR cards may be displayed at an approved table.
  • Private bulletin boards: use only where the owner explicitly permits the material.
Do not tape or staple flyers wherever you find a pole, wall, library, recreation centre, mall or apartment lobby. Get permission and follow the property’s rules. Keep this phase focused on volunteer recruitment only—no orientation or assigning people activities yet.
